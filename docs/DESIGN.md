# The Photocopied Gospel

This is the user-selected visual direction for the current storefront. It supersedes the historical minimal/editorial brief in TINFOIL_HAT_DESIGN.md.

Black ink, off-white photocopied paper, fluorescent yellow headlines/actions, torn edges, staples, distressed editorial atmosphere, a monochrome rain-soaked alley and clearly visible apparel. Restrained red stays inside the original catalog designs. Keep product choices and payment actions readable, even where surrounding art is chaotic.

Display: Anton. Supporting text: IBM Plex Mono. Body: Inter. All sizes and letter spacing are defined in src/style.css. Torn paper is a framing treatment; do not distort the original artwork. Original light/dark print masters stay on S:; WebP derivatives are display assets only.

Four approved featured designs: Black File, Numbers Station, Ancient Tech and Abduction Protocol. Remaining collection designs appear as artwork previews until accurate garment references are connected. Actual fulfillment artwork, garment specifications and prices must match the reviewed Printful product.

The tone is curious, deadpan and darkly playful. Preserve the founding myth, The Signal, The Hat, The All-Seeing Eye, manifesto, collections and existing product microcopy. Fictional lore is presented as brand mythology. The site celebrates curiosity rather than claiming any theory is established fact.
